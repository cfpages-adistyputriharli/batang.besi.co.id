# Batang.Besi.co.id topical-authority map

Last audited: 2026-07-24 (Asia/Jakarta)

## Scope and evidence

`batang.besi.co.id` should teach buyers, fabricators, engineers, and operators how to identify, specify, receive, process, protect, inspect, and procure steel bar products. It may cover round bar/shaft stock, reinforcing bar, square bar (`nako`), and supported adjacent forms, but articles do not replace structural design, metallurgy, welding engineering, standards, or supplier quotations.

- Canonical repository: `cfpages-adistyputriharli/batang.besi.co.id`, `main`.
- 3,570 tracked paths, 2,710 HTML, 16 XML, and one existing Markdown file.
- 2,453 root geographic sales pages: AS S45C 491, AS ST42 490, reinforcing bar 490, threaded square bar 491, and plain square bar 491.
- 246 category/archive HTML routes. Meaningful hubs: `/as-s45c/`, `/as-st42/`, `/besi-beton/`, `/nako/`, and `/nako-ulir/`.
- Distributor-since-2012, factory-price, complete, trusted, cheap, and SNI claims require independent business/product evidence before publication.

No city/province/region briefs. No grade equivalence, chemistry, strength, weldability, bend radius, structural capacity, tolerance, compliance, price, or reuse claim without the exact current standard/product/project evidence and qualified review.

## Cannibalization model

BAR-01 owns definitions; BAR-02 owns form choice; BAR-03 grade meaning; BAR-04 geometry; BAR-05 documentary identity. BAR-06 owns engineering inputs, BAR-07 processing, BAR-08 welding, BAR-09 corrosion, BAR-10 QA, BAR-11 commercial comparison, BAR-12 logistics, BAR-13 lifecycle, and BAR-14 safety/claims. Existing product hubs retain quotation intent. Geographic families are consolidation evidence only.
