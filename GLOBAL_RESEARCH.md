## Target applicability boundary

This catalog concerns solid bar stock and its controlled processing. Base-material records transfer only when the ordered product form, grade, supply condition, heat/batch identity, dimensions, and applicable standard all reconcile.

- Treat `S45C`, `ST42`, assental, shaft, rod, and similar trade labels as search or quotation terms, never as proven equivalence.
- A theoretical mass, nominal diameter, or visual surface condition does not prove delivered tolerance, chemistry, mechanical properties, or suitability.
- Cutting, forming, heat input, welding, machining, coating, and straightening can change the relevant product or material condition; use an approved process and inspection basis.
- Stop before any capacity, fatigue, fire, lifting, repair, or reuse conclusion until the responsible engineer has verified the actual member, connections, history, and service.
